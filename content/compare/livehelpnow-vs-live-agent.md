## TL;DR

- **LiveAgent** publishes clean per-agent rates on annual billing: $15 Small Business, $29 Medium, $49 Large, $69 Enterprise. Both AI features are included at the $15 tier.
- **LiveHelpNow** publishes tiers from $39 Essential, $59 Professional, $79 Premium — every one prefixed "from", with the real number scaling on agents and usage. Its AI layer is a separate purchase: Knowledge AI from $99/month, Conversational AI from $499, Conversational AI Plus from $799.
- LiveAgent covers phone and social; LiveHelpNow does not have a built-in call center. LiveHelpNow's multi-brand support is stronger.
- If you need to compare vendors on a spreadsheet, only one of these lets you.

## Pricing you can model versus pricing you have to ask about

This is the sharpest practical difference. LiveAgent gives you a per-agent number, and headcount times rate gets you close to your invoice. LiveHelpNow's rate card is a floor: a five-agent desk and a fifteen-agent desk both start "from $59" on Professional and are invoiced very differently. There is no way to run a like-for-like comparison against Freshdesk, Help Scout, or LiveAgent without a sales conversation.

That's not automatically bad — usage-based pricing can be fairer for small teams with heavy volume, or the reverse. It just means budget planning requires a written quote for your actual agent count first.

LiveAgent's transparency has a caveat too. The vendor discounts hard and often, and comparison sites republish promotional rates as list; a 33% new-user code was live at the time of writing, putting Small Business at $10 an agent. Budget against $15/$29/$49/$69, because that's what renewal looks like.

## The AI split is the biggest structural difference

LiveAgent includes its AI Answer Assistant and AI Chatbot in Small Business at $15 an agent. That's unusually generous placement — most competitors put AI behind a higher tier or meter it.

LiveHelpNow separates AI into a rate card that operates on a completely different scale from the help desk beneath it:

| Layer | From | Included volume (Professional / Premium) |
|---|---|---|
| AI Agent Assist | $0 | internal agent assistance only |
| Knowledge AI | $99/mo | 2,000 / 5,000 AI answers |
| Conversational AI | $499/mo | 1,000 / 3,000 conversations |
| Conversational AI Plus | $799/mo | 7,000+ conversations |

Conversational AI costs roughly eight times the $59 Professional plan it attaches to. At the entry allowance that's about $0.50 per conversation, which is comfortable if each deflection saves several minutes of agent time and poor if your volume is a few hundred a month.

The free AI Agent Assist is smart packaging and a genuine productivity feature, but it never talks to a customer, so it deflects nothing.

There's a risk difference embedded here too. LiveHelpNow's AI meters on conversation volume, which tracks customer demand rather than your headcount. A campaign, an outage, or a seasonal spike drives the bill upward for reasons outside your control. Model it against your busiest month, not your average one.

## Channel coverage runs LiveAgent's way

LiveAgent consolidates email, live chat, phone, and social — WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram, X, Viber — in one agent workspace, with a built-in call center including IVR and routing from the $29 Medium tier. That call center is the standout: most competitors treat voice as a separate product with its own per-agent fee. Telephony itself is not included, so you bring a VoIP number and pay your carrier for minutes.

Social channels sit on Large Business at $49 or attach to a lower tier as a flat $39/month add-on. Because the add-on is flat and tier upgrades are per agent, the add-on stays cheaper at every realistic team size — 10 agents on Medium plus social is $329 against $490 for Large. Buy Large for SSO, custom roles, and audit logs, not for WhatsApp.

LiveHelpNow's channel story is narrower: live chat, ticketing, knowledge base, support portal, surveys, secure forms, and social channel support, with call logging and Zoom phone integration from Professional. Integration with a phone system is not the same as a call center.

Where LiveHelpNow does pull ahead is multi-brand: it handles support across multiple brands or properties from one account, and Hue AI routes conversations by topic and availability. LiveAgent charges $19 a month per additional knowledge base — cheap for one extra brand, $114 a month at six.

## Two tier traps to know

On LiveHelpNow, ticketing starts at Professional. Essential at $39 is a chat-and-knowledge-base product with no email queue, and almost every support team handles both. The realistic entry point is $59, which also brings the dashboards and data export that make a desk measurable. Branding removal on the widget requires Premium at $79.

On LiveAgent, SLAs and the call center both start at Medium, so if you take phone calls or owe response times your entry price is $29, not $15. Branding removal is free only on Enterprise at $69, which means a consumer brand that wants a clean chat widget jumps to the top tier regardless of what else it needs.

## Who should pick what

- **Small teams needing phone, chat and email in one workspace** → LiveAgent Medium. A built-in call center at $29 an agent is hard to beat.
- **Multi-brand or multi-property support operations** → LiveHelpNow, where the multi-brand handling is native rather than an add-on stack.
- **Anyone running a vendor bake-off on a spreadsheet** → LiveAgent, simply because the numbers are usable.
- **Chat-led desks with high enough volume to justify conversational automation** → LiveHelpNow, but start with Knowledge AI at $99 rather than jumping to $499.
- **Budget-constrained teams that want AI at all** → LiveAgent Small Business at $15, where both AI features are included.
- **Consumer brands that need an unbranded chat widget** → price LiveHelpNow Premium at $79 against LiveAgent Enterprise at $69 per agent; the flat-versus-per-seat difference decides it by headcount.

## Bottom line

LiveAgent is the more predictable purchase and the broader product, with voice as its clearest advantage and a genuinely full entry tier. LiveHelpNow is worth the sales conversation if you run multiple brands or if conversational deflection is a real business case with the volume behind it — but understand that its AI is the product being sold and the help desk is the thing it attaches to.

Get a written quote from LiveHelpNow for your exact agent count before comparing anything, and check liveagent.com/pricing for current list rather than promo rates.
