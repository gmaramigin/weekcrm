## TL;DR

- ProProfs Essentials ($23.88/year) and Business ($47.88/year) are astonishingly cheap and both are **single-user** plans. Its first team tier is Business Complete Elite at $179.97/month for three seats.
- Groove is $12, $20, or $35 per user per month, annual billing only, scaling smoothly from one agent upward.
- ProProfs meters monthly tickets (100 free, 1,000, 6,000, 15,000, 60,000). Groove meters shared inboxes (2, 5, 25). Neither is obvious from the marketing.
- Groove rates 4.3 here against ProProfs' 4.1, and Groove reports 130,000+ support professionals and a 4.8-star user rating.

## The cheap ProProfs tiers are not for teams

This is the single most important thing to understand before comparing headline numbers. ProProfs advertises Essentials at $23.88 and Business at $47.88 per user per year — under five dollars a month for a working help desk with AI drafting, automation rules, and CSAT surveys. Those numbers are real and they are also single-seat licenses.

The first plan that includes colleagues is Business Complete Elite at $179.97 a month for three seats, or roughly $60 per seat. That is ordinary mid-market help desk pricing, and it is about 45 times the annual cost of Business. Nothing sits between them.

Groove has no such discontinuity. Standard is $12 per user, Plus $20, Pro $35, and a two-person team pays exactly twice what a one-person team pays. All three require annual billing — five agents on Standard is $720 committed up front, not $60 a month — but the shape is linear all the way to Standard's 25-user ceiling.

For a solo operator, ProProfs wins on price by an enormous margin. For anyone with two or more agents, that advantage evaporates instantly.

## Pricing

ProProfs, annual: Free (1 user, 100 tickets/month), Essentials $23.88/year (1 user, 1,000 tickets), Business $47.88/year (1 user, 6,000 tickets), Business Complete Elite $179.97/month (3 seats, 15,000 tickets), Customer Delight Suite from $499/month (unlimited users, 60,000 tickets, bundling Live Chat, Knowledge Base, Survey Maker, and Qualaroo). Monthly billing on Essentials and Business is $29.99 either way, which is a fairly blunt push toward annual — Essentials for a full year costs less than one month billed monthly.

Groove, annual: Standard $12/user (2 shared inboxes, email and live chat, one knowledge base, round-robin assignment, capped at 25 users), Plus $20/user (5 inboxes, 10 free Lite Users, SLA management, business hours, AI assist, premium HubSpot/Jira/Salesforce integrations), Pro $35/user (25 inboxes, 50 Lite Users, data exports, enterprise security, skill-based routing, dedicated account manager). AI Draft credits are $0.25 each on top of any plan.

Fifteen agents a year: $2,160 on Groove Standard, $3,600 on Plus, $6,300 on Pro. ProProfs at that headcount means the Customer Delight Suite at $5,988 — which also includes four other products.

## What actually pushes you up a tier

For ProProfs, it is ticket volume. A solo operator at 900 tickets a month pays $23.88 a year; at 1,100 the same person pays $47.88. Both trivial. But two other gates leapfrog several tiers at once: omnichannel — WhatsApp, Facebook, and the rest — appears only at Business Complete Elite, so any consumer brand needing messaging starts at $179.97/month regardless of team size, and SSO and SAML sit at the same level, putting most companies above roughly fifty employees on a $2,160-a-year floor.

For Groove, it is inbox count. Two inboxes covers support@ and one more. Add billing@, partners@, or a regional address and you are at five, which means Plus. Everything else in the tier — SLA management, AI assist, business hours — rides behind that number rather than driving it.

Groove's quieter lever is Lite Users: people who read and comment on conversations without working the queue. Standard includes zero, so every engineer who wants visibility costs a full $12 seat. Plus includes 10 free, Pro includes 50 — worth more than the $8 per-seat gap suggests at any company where product and engineering get looped in.

## AI on both sides, assistive in both cases

Neither does autonomous resolution, so judge both on agent productivity rather than deflection. ProProfs ramps AI through its tiers — 50 response drafts a month plus categorization and grammar checking on Essentials, a first-response generator and tone rewriting on Business, emotion detection and data redaction at Business Complete Elite. Groove splits its AI by pricing model instead: summarization, sentiment, auto-tagging, and writing assist come bundled from Plus upward, while AI Drafts are metered at $0.25 per credit. A five-agent Plus team paying $100 a month in seats that generates 400 drafts adds another $100, pushing the effective seat cost above Pro's list rate.

## Suite bundle versus focused inbox

ProProfs' strongest structural argument is the ecosystem. Native integration with ProProfs Knowledge Base, Chat, Survey Maker, and Qualaroo means no middleware, and the $499 Customer Delight Suite is genuinely efficient if you want all four products. If you only want ticketing, it is a large overspend.

Groove is deliberately narrower: a shared inbox done properly, with collision detection, clean automation, and reporting that is adequate for SMB and thin for enterprise analytics. Native connections to Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Stripe keep context flowing; voice and SMS coverage is limited. One caution before signing — data exports are Pro-only, which on a product whose value is accumulated conversation history is a real lock-in mechanic. Staying on Standard or Plus? Get the export options in writing.

## Who should pick what

- **Freelancers and one-person support functions** → ProProfs. The free tier is a genuine forever product at 100 tickets a month, and Essentials at under $2 a month is close to unbeatable.
- **Two- to five-person teams** → Groove. ProProfs' gap between a single-user tier and a $179.97 three-seat tier has nothing in it for you.
- **Teams with one or two support addresses on a budget** → Groove Standard at $12; count your inboxes honestly first.
- **Companies where non-agents need conversation visibility** → Groove Plus or Pro, for the free Lite User allocations.
- **Organizations wanting help desk, live chat, knowledge base, and surveys from one vendor** → ProProfs Customer Delight Suite at $499/month.
- **Consumer brands needing WhatsApp or Facebook** → ProProfs Business Complete Elite at $179.97/month; the cheap tiers are irrelevant to you.

## Bottom line

These two barely overlap despite sharing a category. ProProfs is optimized for a single operator and for buyers who want the whole ProProfs bundle, with a pricing ladder that jumps sharply the moment you need colleagues, social channels, or SSO. Groove is a focused, well-liked shared inbox with clean per-seat economics from the first agent, at the cost of annual commitment, inbox-driven tiering, and a couple of packaging decisions — metered AI drafts and Pro-gated exports — worth negotiating. Count your agents. If the answer is one, take ProProfs. If it is more than one, take Groove and pick the tier by inbox count.
