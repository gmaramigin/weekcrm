## TL;DR

- The pricing axes do not line up. Simply CRM bills $15/$22/$41 per user per month, annually. Kylas Elevate is a flat $250/month (INR 12,999) for unlimited users and meters records, storage, and features instead.
- Break-even sits around eight to eleven users. Below that Simply CRM Professional is dramatically cheaper; above roughly twenty seats Kylas is not close to being beaten.
- Scope differs too. Simply CRM Professional carries invoicing, quotes, purchase orders, projects, and ticketing. Kylas is a sales CRM — leads, pipeline, mobile, and a long menu of paid add-ons.

## Do the headcount math before anything else

This comparison usually gets decided by arithmetic rather than features, so start there. Simply CRM Professional at $22/user costs $110/month for five people, $440 for twenty, $1,100 for fifty. Kylas Elevate costs $250 at every one of those numbers.

Five users: Simply CRM wins by $140/month. Twelve users: Kylas edges ahead. Twenty-five users: Simply CRM is $550 against Kylas at $250, and by fifty seats the flat plan is a quarter of the price.

The curves also move in different directions over time. A per-seat bill rises when you hire and falls when you do not. Kylas rises when your database grows, which happens continuously — 100,000 records on Elevate is generous and top-ups are only INR 500 per 20,000, but the direction of travel is one-way. Seats you can remove; records you generally cannot.

## Pricing

Simply CRM publishes three annual-only tiers. Starter at $15 covers dashboards, leads, contacts, opportunities, and a visual pipeline, with no automation, email integration, or reporting — not the reason to buy this vendor. Professional at $22 is where the product lives. Enterprise at $41 exists mainly to gate API access.

Kylas has a genuinely free Embark plan with unlimited users and 1,000 non-expandable records — unusual generosity, and equally unusual in having no top-up path. Exceed is quoted, and the trigger for it is database size rather than team size.

The add-on menu is where Kylas stops being flat. Capacity is cheap: storage at INR 300 per 20 GB, records at INR 500 per block. Capability is not: BI analytics at INR 5,000/month, goals at INR 2,500, multi-currency at INR 2,500. Field sales tracking is INR 400 per user per month — the one line item that rebuilds a seat bill inside a seat-free plan, and on a forty-person field team it costs more than the platform.

## Scope: one job or several

Simply CRM Professional includes invoices and quotes, sales orders, purchase orders, price books, service contracts, vendor management, campaigns, webforms, project tracking, and a full helpdesk with tickets and FAQs. At $22 that is closer to a small-business operations suite than a CRM, and it is the honest reason to pick it.

Kylas does not attempt any of that: lead capture and assignment, visual deal management, activity tracking, mobile apps for field reps, workflows. Elevate adds email, SMS, and WhatsApp integration plus API access at the base price — worth flagging, since API access on Simply CRM costs $41 a head.

So a twelve-person team choosing Kylas should budget separately for invoicing and support tooling. The same team on Simply CRM Professional pays $264/month and gets those in the box.

## Region, ecosystem, and terms

Kylas is built for India and comparable emerging markets. Pricing is anchored in rupees, so the dollar figure drifts with the exchange rate, and WhatsApp integration in the base plan signals who the target buyer is. Its integration ecosystem is narrower than Western platforms and feature depth is thinner than Salesforce or HubSpot for complex workflows — the vendor is candid about both. Simply CRM's constraints are similar in kind: limited brand recognition, fewer native integrations, and modules that hit ceilings for power users, offset by 24/7 multilingual support that is rare at $22. Storage limits go unpublished at every tier, worth pinning down before migrating a document-heavy business.

The commitments differ. Simply CRM has no monthly option at all, so a five-person Professional deployment is a $1,320 annual outlay — which is why its 30-day, no-card trial is necessary rather than generous. Kylas trials for only seven days but bills quarterly, and quarterly off-ramps are rarer in this category than they should be. Optional Kylas onboarding runs INR 25,000 one-time with three-hour training at INR 5,000.

## Who should pick what

- **Sales teams of 20+ that keep hiring** → Kylas Elevate. At twenty-five seats it is $10 a head and nothing per-seat competes.
- **Small businesses of 3 to 12 needing CRM plus invoicing plus support** → Simply CRM Professional. Below break-even on headcount and ahead on scope.
- **Four people sitting on a 300,000-record database** → the worst case for Kylas. High cost per head plus record add-ons. Simply CRM at $88/month is the obvious call.
- **Field sales organizations in India** → Kylas, but price the INR 400/user tracking add-on honestly, because it reintroduces the seat bill you thought you had escaped.
- **A very small operation testing the water** → Kylas Embark, free, unlimited users, 1,000 records. Just know there is no expansion path.

## Bottom line

Two competent SMB tools that almost never lose to each other on quality, because the decision is made by structure. If your team is small and your needs are broad, Simply CRM Professional at $22 is straightforward value and the 30-day trial makes the annual commitment safe to test. If your sales floor is growing faster than your database and you already have invoicing handled, Kylas's flat $250 gets cheaper every time you hire — provided you total the capability add-ons first, since BI analytics alone adds a fifth to the base price. Model both at your headcount twelve and twenty-four months out, not just today's, because these two cost curves cross and then keep diverging.
