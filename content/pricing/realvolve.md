## An annual commitment with a two-week window

Realvolve's entry plan is $94 a month billed annually. That phrasing matters: there is no month-to-month option at that rate, so the actual commitment is roughly $1,128 paid for the year rather than a subscription you can cancel next month.

Against that, there is a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. Those two facts define how you should approach buying Realvolve: the trial is not a formality, it is your only opportunity to evaluate before a twelve-month decision.

## Two reported structures

Public pricing information shows two shapes. A flat entry plan at $94 a month on annual billing, and per-agent pricing reported around $59 a month for multi-agent configurations. Realvolve also describes pricing as varying with extra features, deployment type and total user count.

The likeliest reading is that solo agents and small operations buy the flat plan while teams are priced per agent. That distinction changes the comparison considerably.

| Configuration | Structure | Approx. monthly | Approx. annual |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo agent | flat entry plan | $94 | ~$1,128 |
| 3 agents at ~$59 | per agent | ~$177 | ~$2,124 |
| 8 agents at ~$59 | per agent | ~$472 | ~$5,664 |

Ask which model applies to you before benchmarking against competitors, because a flat $94 and $59 per agent produce very different numbers at any team size above one.

## The product you are actually buying

Realvolve's identity is workflow. Not lead capture, not an IDX website, not paid traffic — a checklist and process engine that governs what happens after someone becomes a client. The task sequences, the reminders, the communications that fire at defined points in a transaction.

That matters for pricing because it changes what the money is for. Real estate CRMs broadly split into lead-generation platforms (BoomTown, BoldTrail) that cost four figures a month and bundle paid traffic, and execution tools that cost far less and assume you have leads already. Realvolve is firmly the second kind, and $94 is priced accordingly.

If your problem is not having enough leads, this is the wrong purchase at any price. If your problem is inconsistent follow-through on the leads you have, it is aimed exactly at that.

## The cost nobody quotes

Workflows have to be built. Realvolve's engine is powerful in proportion to how carefully you configure it, and the hours spent designing your listing process, your buyer process and your post-close sequence are a real implementation cost even though nobody invoices you for them.

Budget that time honestly. Agents who subscribe, import contacts and never build a workflow end up paying $1,128 a year for an address book — which is the most common way this product disappoints, and it has nothing to do with the software.

## Making the trial count

Fourteen days is short for a workflow product. Do not spend it importing contacts and clicking around.

Pick one real process — a live listing, from first contact through to closing — and build it end to end. That tells you whether the engine's model of a workflow matches yours, which is the only question that matters and the only one the interface cannot answer on its own. If you reach day fourteen without having built anything, extend the trial or walk away, but do not sign a year on an untested assumption.

## Who should and should not buy this

Realvolve fits individual agents and small teams whose bottleneck is execution rather than lead volume — people with a steady referral or sphere-based business who lose deals to inconsistent follow-up rather than to empty pipelines. The workflow engine is genuinely strong and $94 a month is reasonable for what it does.

It fits poorly for agents who need leads, since Realvolve generates none and the money would be better spent on lead sources. It fits poorly for anyone unwilling to invest configuration time, because an unconfigured Realvolve is an expensive contact list. And it fits poorly for teams that want to pay monthly, since the entry rate requires an annual commitment.

Realvolve's published pricing varies across sources and by configuration. Confirm the structure that applies to your team size at realvolve.com before committing to a year.
