## A price you have to ask for

BoldTrail's own pricing page lists three packages — Base, Plus and Pro — and gives none of them a number. Every one says "Talk to Sales". Everything below comes from reported customer quotes rather than a rate card, which is why the ranges are wide and why your quote may differ.

That structure is common in real estate technology and it has a specific consequence: the price is set by the conversation, not by the product. Two brokerages of similar size regularly pay materially different amounts, and the difference usually reflects how well each one negotiated rather than what each one received.

## What people report paying

| Configuration | Reported monthly | Typical setup fee |
|---|---|---|
| Solo agent | $499–$750 | $500–$1,000 |
| Small team | ~$1,200 | higher, scope-dependent |
| Brokerage | $1,200–$1,800+ | migration-dependent |
| Overall reported range | $299–$1,800 | — |

The $299 figure at the bottom of that range and the $1,800 at the top are both real, which tells you more about the sales process than about the software. Treat the range as a negotiating window, not as a menu.

## Setup fees are the line item people miss

Onboarding charges are near-universal and almost never appear in a monthly-cost comparison. Around $500 to $1,000 for a solo agent; more for teams, scaling with migration volume, training requirements and implementation complexity.

For a solo agent this is the difference between a $499 first month and a $1,400 one. For a brokerage moving years of contact history and transaction records off another platform, it can be several thousand. Ask for it as a separate written figure before you compare BoldTrail against anything else, because competitors that waive onboarding are effectively cheaper by that amount in year one.

## Per-user pricing and the growth problem

BoldTrail charges per user. That is unremarkable until you consider what real estate brokerages actually do: recruit. A quote priced on today's twelve agents is not the bill you will pay when you have twenty.

Before signing a multi-year term, get the per-agent increment in writing and model your realistic roster at the end of the contract, not the start. Brokerages that skip this step discover that a platform they budgeted at $1,400 a month costs $2,300 by year two, entirely through the growth they were celebrating.

## The kvCORE inheritance

BoldTrail is the rebranded successor to kvCORE, and a large share of the pricing information circulating online describes the earlier product. When you compare quotes or read reviews, check the date and the product name — packaging, tiers and rates have all moved through the transition, and a 2023 kvCORE figure is not a useful anchor for a 2026 BoldTrail negotiation.

## How to approach the quote

Three things give you leverage in a no-list-price negotiation.

Specificity: arrive with an exact agent count, a growth projection and a list of the features you actually need, rather than asking what the packages cost. Separation: insist on the monthly rate, the setup fee and the per-agent increment as three distinct numbers. Comparison: get a parallel quote from Follow Up Boss, Sierra Interactive or Lofty on the same roster, because the only pressure available against an unpublished price is a competing one.

## Who should and should not buy this

BoldTrail fits teams and brokerages that want an all-in-one platform — IDX website, CRM, lead capture, behavioural automation — rather than assembling three tools, and that have enough agents to justify a four-figure monthly commitment. The behavioural automation and lead-nurture engine are the reasons people pay, and at brokerage scale they generally justify it.

It fits poorly for solo agents on a tight budget, where $499 to $750 a month plus setup is a serious commitment against alternatives at a fraction of the cost. It also fits poorly for anyone who needs to know the price before taking a sales call — BoldTrail has decided that is not how it sells, and no amount of research produces a quote.

All figures here are reported customer pricing, not published rates. Confirm current packages, setup fees and per-agent terms directly with BoldTrail before budgeting.
