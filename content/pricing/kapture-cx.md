## One published price, three editions

Kapture CX sits between the vendors that publish everything and the vendors that publish nothing. Essential is $39 per user per month, and that number is public. Professional and Enterprise are quoted.

What Kapture does say about the model is unusually specific: the platform is modular, and you pay only for what you use, with pricing shaped by product, users, interaction volumes and AI features. Four variables, only one of which — users — is the thing a per-seat price normally describes.

## What $39 actually includes

The Essential feature list is the strongest argument for Kapture on price. Ticketing, 1000+ API integrations, Agent Assist, Customer 360, knowledge base, SLA and TAT management, side conversations, analytics and online support.

SLA management at the entry tier is the notable one. Several major competitors gate it behind their second or third plan, which means a $39 Kapture seat and a $39 competitor seat are frequently not the same purchase. If SLA and turnaround-time tracking is a requirement — and for any support team with commitments to customers it is — this changes the comparison materially.

Customer 360 at entry level is the second. A unified customer view is often positioned as premium functionality elsewhere.

## The variable nobody models

Interaction volume is named explicitly as a pricing factor. That is the sentence to focus on, because it means Kapture is not purely a per-seat product.

Two support teams of fifteen agents — one handling 3,000 tickets a month, the other 30,000 — are not necessarily paying the same per-seat rate on the same edition. The published $39 is an anchor, not a guarantee, and the quote you receive will reflect your volume.

The practical response is to lead with your volume rather than have it discovered. Bring twelve months of actual interaction counts to the conversation and ask for the rate that applies at that volume, so you are comparing a real number against competitors rather than a headline you may not qualify for.

## AI as a separate layer

The fourth variable is AI features. Kapture lists them as something that shapes pricing rather than something included in a tier, which strongly implies separate charging.

Ask for AI as its own line item. This matters beyond budgeting: a blended per-seat quote that includes AI makes it impossible to evaluate whether the AI is earning its cost, and impossible to remove it if it is not. Support AI in 2026 varies enormously in quality between vendors, and you want the ability to price it, trial it and drop it independently of the platform.

## The upgrade path is unclear from outside

Public feature lists for Essential, Professional and Enterprise overlap substantially — all three list ticketing, 1000+ API integrations and online support, with Professional adding multilingual and 24x7 support and Enterprise adding Customer 360 and the analytics dashboard that Essential already appears to include.

That is confusing enough that you should not attempt to choose a tier from public information. Ask for a written feature delta: what specifically does Professional have that Essential does not, and what does Enterprise add. Without that, an upgrade recommendation is impossible to evaluate.

## Who should and should not buy this

Kapture CX fits mid-market customer support operations, particularly in retail, travel, BFSI and consumer services where sector-specific workflow configuration matters. The Essential feature set is genuinely strong for $39, and the stated absence of contract lock-in is a real advantage against competitors that require annual prepayment.

It fits poorly for buyers who need to compare prices without a sales conversation, since three of the four pricing variables are only resolved in a quote. It fits poorly for very high-volume operations that want per-seat certainty, since interaction volume moves the rate. And it fits poorly for small teams wanting a self-serve signup, which is not how this product is sold.

Kapture prices modularly and only Essential is published. Confirm your per-user rate, volume assumptions and AI pricing directly at kapture.cx before budgeting.
