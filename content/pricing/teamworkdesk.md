## Cheap entry, conventional ladder, one hard cap

Teamwork Desk's headline rate of $9.50 per agent per month on annual billing is among the lowest in the hosted help desk market — well under Freshdesk and Zendesk at comparable positions. For a small support team it is a genuinely inexpensive way to stop running support out of a shared mailbox.

The tier structure that follows is conventional, with one structural constraint that separates it from ordinary feature-gating: **Starter is capped at 10 agents.**

## The agent cliff

| Plan | Annual rate | Agents | 10 agents/mo | 11 agents/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $9.50 | Max 10 | $95 | not available |
| Pro | $17 | Unlimited | $170 | $187 |
| Premium | $33 | Unlimited | $330 | $363 |

Hiring an eleventh support agent takes a team from $95/month to $187/month — a 97% increase in the bill for a 10% increase in headcount, because the eleventh seat forces the entire desk onto Pro.

This is the same shape of cliff that catches Attio customers at ten users, and it deserves the same response: if you are at eight or nine agents and growing, price Pro now. Budgeting against Starter when you are near its ceiling means planning around a number you are about to lose.

## Where the features actually sit

Two gates matter more than the rest.

**Live chat starts at Pro.** Starter covers email ticketing, ticket management, a free help docs site and a customer portal. It does not cover chat. For a modern support desk — particularly consumer-facing or SaaS — chat is not an advanced feature, and its placement at $17 means the practical entry price for a full channel mix is nearly double the headline.

**SLAs start at Premium.** So do customer satisfaction ratings, custom fields, full reporting, business hours and scheduled tickets. That cluster is the difference between running a support function and merely answering mail: if you have committed to response times, or need to report on how support is performing, you are at $33 per agent — 3.5x the advertised rate.

The free help docs site included at Starter is a real bright spot worth naming. Self-service deflection is often gated to mid-tiers elsewhere, and having it at the entry level is genuinely generous.

## Annual billing is worth more than usual

Teamwork advertises annual billing as saving 29% against monthly — meaningfully better than the 20% category norm, and correspondingly, monthly billing costs about 41% more than the annual rate.

On a ten-agent Pro deployment that is roughly $840 a year for flexibility. Support headcount is typically more predictable than sales headcount, which makes this an easy decision for most desks: take the annual term.

The 30-day trial with no credit card required also gives real room to evaluate — twice the common 14 days, and enough to run actual ticket volume through the product before committing.

## The Teamwork.com question

Teamwork Desk's distinguishing feature is not price and not any individual capability. It is the connection to Teamwork.com projects, which lets a support ticket become tracked project work without re-keying it into a separate system. Time tracking at Pro reinforces this: the product assumes billable work sometimes originates in support.

For agencies and professional services firms already running Teamwork for client projects, that integration is the whole argument and it is a strong one — the alternative is a help desk and a project tool that do not know about each other.

For a buyer with no Teamwork footprint, the integration is worth nothing, and Teamwork Desk becomes a mid-sized help desk competing on ordinary merit against Freshdesk, Help Scout and Zendesk. It holds up reasonably at Pro, but the price advantage narrows once you account for what those competitors include at similar tiers.

## Who should and should not buy this

Teamwork Desk fits agencies and services firms already on Teamwork.com, where ticket-to-project continuity removes real friction. It also fits small support teams under ten agents handling email-first volume, for whom Starter at $9.50 with a free docs site is excellent value.

It fits badly for teams that need SLAs or reporting on a budget, since both sit at $33; for teams approaching ten agents, who should model the cliff before it arrives; and for healthcare buyers, since HIPAA verification is Enterprise-only and puts them into custom pricing immediately.
