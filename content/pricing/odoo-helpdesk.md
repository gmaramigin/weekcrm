## The most generous free tier in help desk software, with one rule

Odoo's One App Free plan does something no dedicated help desk vendor will match: it gives you Helpdesk, in full, for **unlimited users**, hosted and maintained, at no cost, forever.

Not five agents. Not a 14-day trial. Not a feature-stripped community edition. The whole application, for as many people as you like, with hosting and support included.

The condition is exactly one: Helpdesk must be the only Odoo app you run.

For an organisation that wants a standalone ticketing system and nothing more, this is close to unbeatable, and it is worth stating plainly because it sounds too good to be accurate. Freshdesk, Zendesk and Help Scout all charge per agent from the first seat. Odoo charges nothing.

## The cliff, not the ladder

The catch is not hidden, but its shape is easy to underestimate. Odoo does not price per app. It prices per *user*, once you cross from one app to two.

| Configuration | Cost for 20 users/year |
|---|---|
| Helpdesk only | $0 |
| Helpdesk + CRM | $1,740 |
| Helpdesk + CRM + Accounting | $1,740 |
| All apps, Custom tier | $2,616 |

The step from row one to row two is the entire pricing decision. Adding a single second app takes a twenty-person company from zero to $1,740 a year — and adding the third, fourth and tenth app costs nothing further.

This is a deliberate and rather elegant design. Odoo is not really selling Helpdesk; it is using Helpdesk as an entry point into the suite. The free tier is engineered to be outgrown, because a support team that cannot see the customer's CRM record, open invoice or shipment status is a support team with a real problem, and the fix costs $7.25 per user per month.

Most support desks hit that wall quickly. Ticket context lives in other systems, and the second app is usually a matter of when rather than whether.

## Once you are paying, it is very cheap

The important counterpoint: Standard at $7.25 per user per month annually buys **all** the apps. CRM, Sales, Inventory, Accounting, Projects, HR, Manufacturing, Point of Sale — hundreds of them, with hosting, maintenance and unlimited support included.

Compared with buying a help desk, a CRM and an accounting package separately, this is not a close contest on licence cost. A twenty-person business running five Odoo apps pays $1,740 a year in total. The equivalent best-of-breed stack would run several times that.

Monthly billing costs $8.95 against $7.25 annually — a 23% premium, roughly the market norm.

## What forces the Custom tier

Three triggers move you from $7.25 to $10.90, a 50% increase:

- **External API access** — any programmatic integration with a system outside Odoo
- **Odoo Studio** — the customisation tool for modifying models, views and workflows
- **Hosting choice** — Standard is Odoo Online only; Odoo.sh and on-premise require Custom

The Studio gate is the one that catches people. Odoo's appeal is substantially about adapting the system to how your business actually works, and Studio is how that is done without a developer. Teams that buy Standard intending to run stock configuration frequently discover, three months in, that they need one workflow changed — and that change costs 50% more per user across the entire company.

If you expect to customise anything, budget Custom from the start.

## The cost that dwarfs the subscription

Implementation is quoted separately at every tier, through Odoo's Success Packs or a certified partner: project management, business needs analysis, data import, customisations.

This is where Odoo deployments actually cost money. For anything beyond a simple single-app setup, professional services routinely exceed the first year of licensing — sometimes by a wide margin. A multi-app rollout with data migration from legacy systems is a project, not a signup.

The low per-user pricing is genuine and it is also the most misleading thing about Odoo's total cost of ownership. Treat the subscription as the smallest line in the budget for any serious deployment.

## Who should and should not buy this

Odoo Helpdesk is an outstanding choice for teams wanting standalone ticketing with no budget — the free tier is real, unlimited and permanent, and nothing else in the category comes close. It is equally strong for businesses that want a full operational suite at low licence cost and have the appetite to implement it.

It fits badly for teams that want a polished, specialised support experience without adopting a wider platform: Odoo Helpdesk is a competent module inside an ERP, not a best-in-class support product, and it will not match Zendesk or Intercom on support-specific depth. And it fits badly for anyone who reads $7.25 as the cost of an Odoo project, since implementation is where the real spend lives.

Confirm current rates at odoo.com/pricing and get an implementation quote before comparing total cost against dedicated help desk vendors.
