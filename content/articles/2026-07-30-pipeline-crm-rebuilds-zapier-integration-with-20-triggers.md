Pipeline CRM has rebuilt its Zapier integration, roughly tripling the number of automations users can create. The updated integration introduces around 20 triggers that fire on deal changes, closings, and deletions — not just deal creation. It also adds two-way calendar sync, document upload support, and access to custom fields in every Zap step. Existing Zaps continue to run without requiring manual updates. The expanded integration is aimed at teams that rely on Zapier to connect Pipeline CRM with other tools in their stack and need more granular workflow automation around deal activity.
