Vendasta has released a new AI-powered onboarding experience for early access partners that replaces the traditional welcome email with a summary of work already completed for each client's business. Instead of an empty dashboard, new clients receive an onboarding email and an in-app home screen card showing AI-generated results — including gathered and analyzed reviews, directory scans across 40+ listings, a prefilled business profile, and a website built by the company's Vibe tool when active. Each summary is populated from real account data and adapts to the products enabled. The home screen also presents recommended next steps and one-click upgrade cards for additional AI products. Summary items automatically retire roughly 30 days after completion. Partners can join the early access program through Partner Center under Administration > Customize > General Product Settings.
